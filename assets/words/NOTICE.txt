Filter applied for Lafz: single-token words of exactly 5 Urdu graphemes,
using the in-game keyboard alphabet (plus common hamza variants).
Phrases with spaces were excluded. Three curated compounds from the
previous Lafz list were kept: ہمراہ، ہمسفر، ہمراز.
